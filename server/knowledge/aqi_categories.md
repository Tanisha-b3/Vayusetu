# AQI categories and health meaning (CPCB-style, India)

## Overview of bands
The Indian National AQI uses six bands: Good (0-50), Satisfactory (51-100), Moderate (101-200), Poor (201-300), Very Poor (301-400), Severe (401-500). A band describes the likely health concern, not a prediction for any one person.

## Good and Satisfactory
Good: minimal impact. Satisfactory: may cause minor breathing discomfort to sensitive people. Normal activity is fine for most people; sensitive people should watch for symptoms.

## Moderate
May cause breathing discomfort to people with lung disease such as asthma, and to people with heart disease, children and older adults. Sensitive groups should reduce long or heavy outdoor exertion.

## Poor
May cause breathing discomfort to most people on prolonged exposure, and discomfort to people with heart disease. Everyone should reduce prolonged outdoor exertion; sensitive groups should limit time outdoors.

## Very Poor
May cause respiratory illness on prolonged exposure. Avoid outdoor exertion. Children, older adults and people with heart or lung conditions should stay indoors as much as possible.

## Severe
Affects healthy people and seriously affects people with existing disease. Avoid all outdoor physical activity. Public health advisories and emergency measures are typically considered.

## PM2.5 and PM10 reference bands (24-hour, ug/m3)
PM2.5: Good 0-30, Satisfactory 31-60, Moderate 61-90, Poor 91-120, Very Poor 121-250, Severe above 250. PM10: Good 0-50, Satisfactory 51-100, Moderate 101-250, Poor 251-350, Very Poor 351-430, Severe above 430. PM2.5 particles are finer and reach deeper into the lungs. A single spot reading is not the same as a 24-hour average, so say "reading" not "average" unless the data says so.
