# Health precautions for citizen advisories (general guidance, not medical advice)

## General public
On Poor or worse days: reduce long outdoor exercise, prefer indoor activity, avoid outdoor burning of waste or leaves, keep windows closed during the worst hours if possible, and use wet mopping instead of dry sweeping indoors. A well-fitted N95/FFP2-type mask can reduce exposure to fine particles when going outside; thin cloth or surgical masks give limited protection.

## Children
Children breathe faster and spend more time outdoors, so they are more exposed. On Moderate days limit heavy outdoor exertion for children with asthma. On Poor or worse days move sports and play indoors, avoid outdoor assemblies and long commutes on foot, and ensure children with asthma carry their prescribed inhalers. Tell a parent or teacher if a child coughs, wheezes or has trouble breathing.

## Older adults
Older adults are more likely to have heart or lung conditions. On Poor or worse days stay indoors as much as possible, avoid morning walks in heavy haze, keep regular medicines available, and keep a family member or neighbour informed. Seek medical help for chest pain, severe breathlessness or fainting.

## People sensitive to poor air
This includes people with asthma, COPD, heart disease, pregnant women and people recovering from illness. Follow your doctor's action plan, keep rescue medicines within reach, reduce outdoor exposure earlier (from Moderate), and watch for symptoms such as wheezing, persistent cough, chest tightness or unusual tiredness.

## When to seek medical help
Seek medical advice promptly for difficulty breathing, chest pain, persistent wheezing, dizziness or confusion, or symptoms that do not improve indoors. In an emergency call the local emergency number. This advisory is general guidance and does not replace a doctor's advice.

## Indoor air tips
Avoid burning incense, mosquito coils, wood or waste indoors. A HEPA air purifier in one room can help if available. Cooking with good ventilation lowers indoor particles.
